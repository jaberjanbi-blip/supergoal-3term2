From my mistakes, I learned to think before I act.

💡 Writing Tips

• Start your topic sentence with a capital letter.
• Use "have made" for life experience, and past simple (helped, taught) for specific events.
• End every sentence with a full stop.
• Try to use at least 2 lesson words.
