Part الجزءWhat It DoesFrom the Paragraph
🔵 Topic Sentence

Opens the letter and states the main idea
تفتتح الرسالة وتحدد الفكرة الرئيسية
I hope you are well, and I want to tell you about my trip to China.
🟢 Supporting Sentences
الجمل الداعمة
Give details and examples
تعطي تفاصيل وأمثلة
Life here is fast… Many people ride bicycles… The food is different…
🟣 Concluding Sentence
جملة الخاتمة
Closes the letter warmly
تختم الرسالة بأسلوب ودّي
I am learning a lot and having a good time, and I look forward to telling you more when I return.

💡 Writing Tips

• Start your topic sentence with a capital letter.
• Don't forget the letter format: "Dear [Name]," at the start and "Your friend," + your name at the end.
• End every sentence with a full stop.
• Try to use at least 2 lesson words.
