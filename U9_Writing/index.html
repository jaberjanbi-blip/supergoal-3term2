🧩 Vocabulary Reminders

💡 Writing Tips

• Start your topic sentence with a capital letter.
• After could/might/may, always use the base form of the verb (e.g. "might give," not "might gives").
• End every sentence with a full stop.
• Try to use at least 2 lesson words.
